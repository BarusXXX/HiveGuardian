## Generated artwork and voices

- Splash artwork: created with ChatGPT and supplied by Kane Borg for Hive Guardian.
- Hive timber: generated photographic-style cedar texture using OpenAI image generation.
  It is not a measured material scan.
- Maltese bee performances: generated using ElevenLabs on a paid plan with services
  outside beta. Voice models are not distributed with the game.
- Garden ambience, hive hum and insect effects: synthesized game audio.

## Music

Original game pieces include *Honey Light* and *Hive Under Siege*.
The hornet music arranges Rimsky-Korsakov's *Flight of the Bumblebee*, a public-domain composition.

Instrument samples: **FluidR3_GM General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen**, released under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Converted to individual MP3 notes by
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).
The game bundles only the notes used in its soundtrack.

## Landscape materials

Photographic materials from **Poly Haven**, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license):

- [Dry Ground Rocks](https://polyhaven.com/a/dry_ground_rocks): exposed soil, gravel paths and soil pockets.
- [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock): low ground cover around the hive.
- [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry): surface detail on modelled limestone and rubble walls.
- [Bark Willow](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow): photographed bark used as a proxy for olive bark.

These are photographic material references, not scans captured in Malta.
The Maltese terrain, plants and dry-stone construction are modelled separately.

## Olive tree

The flowering olive is adapted from assets released under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- **Innerscene** — [Mature olive tree](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/mature-olive-tree-bc261a43).
  Source for the connected trunk, roots and branches; original foliage replaced.
- **Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven** — [Shrub 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_04).
  Source leafy shoots and photographic foliage maps, reshaped into olive-like leaves.
- **Poly Haven** — [Bark Willow](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow).
  Photographic bark proxy; the original photograph depicts willow.

The game includes adapted Low, Medium and High detail models.
This is an olive adaptation of open assets, not a scan of a Maltese olive tree.
